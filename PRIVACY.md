# Sukuro Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 27, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Sukuro**. We take your privacy very seriously. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Data we collect
We collect the following types of data to ensure the proper functioning of the app:

*   **Account information (Authentication):** When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and a unique user identifier. This data is managed through our authentication provider, **Supabase**.
*   **Progress data:** We store your learning progress (completed lessons, scores, learned vocabulary) in our database in order to synchronize your experience across your devices.
*   **Technical data (Logs):** Technical information (device type, operating system version, crash reports) may be collected automatically to help us fix bugs and improve the app.

## 3. Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Sukuro uses **Google Gemini (Google Generative AI)** services to generate educational content, corrections, or example sentences.
*   Text data (e.g. your answers to an exercise) may be sent to Google servers to be processed.
*   This data is not used by Google to train their models (according to their standard API terms of use) and is used only to provide the immediate response in the app.

## 4. Sharing data with third parties
We never sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers who help us operate the app:

*   **Supabase:** For database hosting and user authentication.
*   **Google (Firebase/Cloud):** For authentication (Google Sign-In) and AI services (Gemini).
*   **Apple:** For authentication (Sign in with Apple) on iOS devices.

## 5. Data security
We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. All communications between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS/SSL). Access to your account is protected by the secure authentication protocols of Google and Apple.

## 6. Data retention and deletion
We retain your data as long as your account is active.
In line with Google Play Store requirements, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data.
To request deletion of your account, you can:
1. Use the \"Delete my account\" option in the app settings (if available), or
2. Contact us directly at the following address: **florian.budniewski@hotmail.com**.

## 7. Children
Our app is intended for language learning for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
